Malika ... by Jamie

Malika More is a researcher who gave a seminar I was at. She has a wonderfully mobile face and I was captivated by the play of expressions across it while she took a question from the audience.

Why the colours? Low light flattened the originals (below) and made them boring (in my opinion), so I cranked up the contrast to bring out ‘the reality behind the image’. In the first column the expressions follow the order in which we, the audience, enjoyed them.
