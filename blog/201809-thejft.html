It’s an exciting time to be involved in academic publishing, and on that topic I am happy to announce the creation of the Journal of Financial Technology (the JFT).

Thank you to a varied and expert editorial board who have joined up for this open-access journal, founded to develop the fusion of mathematics, statistics, computer science, finance, and law in the new field of financial technology (fintech).

Publication is free and open-access online to authors and readers. Print copies will be available from Amazon. Authors retain copyright of their work.

Fintech is a social, computational, and mathematical enterprise, and the excellent editorial board reflects this. Quality is high and there is an editorial emphasis on serving science and fintech by bringing insightful, rigorous, well-written papers to fruition.

To submit an article please e-mail your submission to Jane Spurr (jane.spurr@kcl.ac.uk) as per the Journal’s submissions procedure.
